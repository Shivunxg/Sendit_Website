1. About This Policy

This Privacy Policy is published by SendIt Logistics Private Limited (CIN: U74999KA2025PTC123456), a company incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013, with its registered office at 123, Outer Ring Road, Kadubeesanahalli, Bengaluru – 560 103, Karnataka, India ("SendIt", "we", "us", or "our").

SendIt operates the SwiftShip courier orchestration platform, the WareSync warehouse management system, and provides third-party logistics (3PL) services. This Policy is framed in compliance with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDPA 2023").

2. Data We Collect

2.1 Account and Business Data

When you register for SwiftShip or WareSync, we collect your name, business name, GST number, work email address, mobile number, billing address, and payment instrument details (stored via our PCI-DSS-compliant payment processor).

2.2 Shipment and Consignee Data

In the course of processing shipments, we collect and process the following data about end consignees: full name, delivery address (including PIN code), mobile number or email address for delivery notifications, order value (for COD purposes), and package contents description.

2.3 Rider and Fleet Location Data

For last-mile delivery operations, we collect real-time GPS location data from our rider application. This data is used solely for route optimisation, live tracking provision, COD accountability, and operational performance monitoring.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use personal data for: providing and operating the platforms; fulfilling 3PL service obligations; processing payments and COD reconciliation; sending shipment tracking notifications; conducting security monitoring; complying with applicable law; and improving our services through aggregate analytics.

4. Third-Party Sharing

We share personal data with: carrier partners (shipment data for delivery purposes); payment processors (Razorpay, Cashfree); cloud infrastructure providers (AWS Mumbai region); communication service providers (SMS/WhatsApp notifications); and regulatory authorities when required by applicable Indian law. We do not sell personal data to any third party.

5. Data Retention

Shipment and consignee data is retained for 7 years from the date of shipment. Account data is retained for the duration of the customer relationship and for 7 years thereafter. Rider location data is retained in raw form for 90 days and in aggregated form for 3 years.

6. Your Rights Under DPDPA 2023

You have the right to access, correction, erasure, and grievance redressal in respect of your personal data. To exercise any of these rights, contact our DPO at dpo@sendit.in. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Cookies

Our website uses strictly necessary cookies (session management, security) and analytics cookies (Google Analytics 4, deployed only with consent). Advertising and tracking cookies are not deployed on our Platforms.

8. Security

We implement ISO 27001-aligned controls including TLS 1.2+ encryption in transit, AES-256 encryption at rest, role-based access controls, multi-factor authentication, and quarterly penetration testing.
